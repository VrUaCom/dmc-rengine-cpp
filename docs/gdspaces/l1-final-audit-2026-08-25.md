## 4. Cross-layer completion seam

Merged evidence proves normal `0x1401B8DC0` receives only one registry-relative u32 context. It cannot inspect raw transport status itself.

After direct L1 terminal semantics are exact, prove how the runtime converts them into an allowed or suppressed L3 normal completion. Relevant evidence includes `0x1402EF4D0`, scheduler behavior in `0x1402EF790`, fresh `0x1400333E0/0x140033390`, `0x1400335A0`, relevant `0x1402EF460`, and `.lst` recursive failure ordering.
