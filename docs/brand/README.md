# DMC Rengine Brand Canon

This directory contains the public brand layer of DMC Rengine.

## Canon documents

- [Lore](lore.md) — The Order of the Inverted Triangle, Monks of Reverse, the Reverse Canon, Spider Hub, the Sealed Archives, The Sect of Neuroslop, and The Long Descent.
- [The Sect of Neuroslop](sect-of-neuroslop.md) — the satirical AI-assisted community layer, its doctrine, humorous ranks, slogans, symbols, and safeguards.
- [Naming System](naming-system.md) — canonical marketing and subsystem names.
- [Voice and Marketing](voice-and-marketing.md) — positioning, taglines, community ranks, visual language, and prohibited claims.

## Separation of concerns

Brand terminology may enrich interfaces, presentations, community pages, release notes, and onboarding. It must not obscure technical contracts.

Examples:

- `GDSpaces` remains the API and namespace name; **The Archive** is its marketing alias.
- `EXE Editor` remains the product name; **The Scriptorium** is its lore alias.
- `Binary Inspector` remains the product name; **The Reliquary** is its lore alias.
- `Stage Ops` remains the product name; **The Theatre** is its lore alias.
- `ModViz` remains the product name; **The Observatory** is its lore alias.
- **The Sect of Neuroslop** is a satirical community identity and never replaces technical review, evidence, repository permissions, or contributor responsibility.

Technical code, schemas, tests, and APIs must always use canonical engineering names.

## Canon status

The technical product names predate this repository and are treated as established project canon. The Order, Monks of Reverse, Sect of Neuroslop, mottos, ranks, and extended mythology in this directory define the public C++-era brand canon of the open repository.

All references to an Order, sect, monks, doctrine, heresy, or canon are fictional brand language. DMC Rengine is an open technical research project, not a religious or coercive organization.
