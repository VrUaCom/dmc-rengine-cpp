## Validation authority rule

L1/L2/L3 may report implementation/reverse states such as `implemented`, `bounded-closed`, `real-receipt-required` and `evidence-submitted`.

Only V may promote a declared scope to:

- `original-equivalent`;
- `game-ready-equivalent`;
- `COMPLETE`;
- `100%` when used as a completion claim.

Evidence classes remain separate:

- **V-A:** provenance/artifact/receipt/trusted-origin integrity;
- **V-B:** product CI and controlled tests;
- **V-C:** exact real-corpus validation;
- **V-D:** trusted original-process equivalence;
- **V-E:** breadth/subsystem acceptance.

Three independent layer-local PASS results are not one vertical proof. V must bind the same `validation_run_id`, resource identity, selected provider, bytes and original consumer/lifecycle chain.
